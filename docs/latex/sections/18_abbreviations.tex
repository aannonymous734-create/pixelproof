\multicolumn{2}{|l|}{\textit{Earth observation and imagery}}\\
\hline
EO & Earth observation\\
\hline
Sentinel-2 & the Copernicus optical satellite pair whose patches EuroSAT is built from\\
\hline
EuroSAT & the public dataset of 27,000 labelled Sentinel-2 patches (64$\times$64, 10 land-cover classes)\\
\hline
Patch / tile & a 64$\times$64 image; larger images are cut into tiles, never resized\\
\hline
Resampling & rescaling or rotating an image by interpolation; leaves periodic pixel correlations\\
\hline
Splicing & pasting a region from another image\\
\hline
Copy-move & duplicating a region inside the same image\\
\hline
Inpainting & erasing a region and filling it from its surroundings (classical or learned)\\
\hline
Laundering & post-processing (re-compression, blur, noise) that hides forensic traces\\
\hline
\multicolumn{2}{|l|}{\textit{Security and provenance}}\\
\hline
Digest & a SHA-256 fingerprint of the pixels; any pixel change alters it\\
\hline
Ed25519 & an elliptic-curve signature scheme with deterministic signatures\\
\hline
Claim / manifest & the signed statement of origin: source, sensor, capture date, digest\\
\hline
Processing chain & the signed, linked record of every processing step\\
\hline
Registry & the ground station's list of trusted public keys, with roles and revocation flags\\
\hline
Replay & re-sending a genuine, already delivered message\\
\hline
MITM & man-in-the-middle: an attacker on the path between sender and receiver\\
\hline
DNAT & destination network-address translation (the iptables rule of Lab D2)\\
\hline
PKCS\#11, HSM, TPM & interfaces and devices for hardware-backed keys (upgrade path of the trust anchor)\\
\hline
STRIDE & Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information disclosure, Denial of service, Elevation of privilege\\
\hline
JWT & JSON Web Token (the sign-in tokens)\\
\hline
TLS & transport-layer security (not enabled in the isolated lab)\\
\hline
LSB & least-significant bit (hidden-data embedding)\\
\hline
