\section{Objectives}\label{sec:objectives}
\begin{keybox}
\textbf{Main objective.} Prove where every pixel came from, assess what it shows without over-claiming, never silently release a doubtful patch, keep the analyst in charge of releasing quarantined data, and make every decision auditable, on a laptop with no GPU.
\end{keybox}
The challenge asks for reported detection quality, false alarms, latency, memory and security overhead; it sets no numeric targets. The {{m:/target_fpr|pct0}} false-alarm budget is ours and is fixed on validation; the other targets are stated qualitatively and measured on frozen sets.
